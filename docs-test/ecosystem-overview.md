# Ecosystem

Making robots is hard. It really takes a team of specialists to make a better robot. As a researcher, prototyper, or student, you likely specialize in one area \- but building a robot requires expertise across three [Domains](/test/docs/domain-overview/):

1. [**Mechanical**](/test/docs/domain-mechanical/) \- Structures must fit together and align properly.  
2. [**Electrical**](/test/docs/domain-electrical/) \- Power and data must route correctly between components.  
3. [**Computational**](/test/docs/domain-computational/) \- Software must communicate with hardware reliably.

What if you could focus on your expertise while trusting that the other domains are already solved? These problems should be solved once and shared \- not repeated by every team, every time.

**SPOKE** is a composable robotics system: a hybrid open-closed ecosystem designed for modularity, scalability, and collaboration. It combines the reliability of curated components, the extensibility of open-source collaboration, and the power of community-driven innovation, thereby enabling rapid assembly, modification, and scaling of robotic systems.

AI is not an add-on \- it's integrated into the system. From control logic to full autonomy, **SPOKE** supports pre-trained models, swappable algorithms, and community contributions. With integrated AI capabilities, the system supports learning algorithms, perception models, and autonomous behaviors \- ready to evolve alongside your robot. **SPOKE** supports shared intelligence as easily as it supports shared parts. 

Every **SPOKE** Part is verified for mechanical fit, electrical compatibility, and computational interfaces before entering the ecosystem. **SPOKE** Sets are complete robotic designs made from these Parts. Select a Set appropriate for your task, then when you assemble the robot, mechanical interfaces align, electrical connections work, and software recognizes the configuration \- all without custom integration work. You can also easily adjust, modify or add Parts as needed. Or design your own robot from scratch.

The **SPOKE** Composer software will verify your new robot design before you build, create a parts list, and a set of assembly instructions. This enables anyone to create new configurations, share robot designs, and contribute AI models built on this reliable foundation.
